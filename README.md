Recommended Analysis (Real-world Framing)
1.	Identified peak sales hours by analyzing hourly trends of footfall and quantity sold — helping optimize staffing and promotions during high-traffic hours.
2.	Evaluated weekly performance of the store by comparing footfall and total sales across each day — to identify which weekdays drive the highest revenue.
3.	Analyzed store-wise performance (Lower Manhattan, Hell’s Kitchen, Astoria) to identify the most profitable location based on footfall and total sales.
4.	Assessed average customer behavior using metrics like average bill per person and average orders per person — useful for planning up-selling and combo offers.
5.	Studied order distribution by product size (Large, Regular, Small) to understand customer preference and product packaging strategy.
6.	Identified top-selling product categories (Barista Espresso, Brewed Chai Tea, etc.) based on both sales revenue and quantity sold.
7.	Visualized category-wise contribution to overall sales — highlighting that Coffee and Tea dominate the product mix, which guides inventory and marketing focus.

📌 Insights Extracted from the Dashboard
⏰ Peak Sales Hours Insight:

The highest footfall and quantity sold occurred between 9 AM to 11 AM, peaking at 26,713 units sold at 11 AM. generally 10am is a piak time 
Suggests a strong morning coffee rush — ideal time slot for targeted promotions or discounts.

📅 Weekday Performance Insight:

Monday and Thursday showed the highest sales (over $1,01,000) and consistent footfall (~21,600), making them the most profitable weekdays. friday the footfall is high and monday has a sales high commpare  to friday.
Sunday and Saturday had the lowest sales and footfall, indicating reduced weekend performance.

📍 Store-wise Performance Insight:

Hell’s Kitchen outperformed others with 50,735 footfalls and $2,36,511 in total sales.
Closely followed by Astoria, while Lower Manhattan had the lowest footfall and sales — useful for resource reallocation or improvement planning.

👥 Customer Behavior Insight:

Average bill per person is $4.69, and average orders per person is 1.44.
Indicates customers are spending moderately and buying slightly more than one item — good scope for promoting combo offers or loyalty programs.

📦 Product Size Preference Insight:

Majority of the orders are for Large (34%) and Regular (28%) sizes.
Only 7% of customers opt for Small, suggesting a strong preference for larger servings — can guide packaging, pricing, and inventory.

🏆 Top-Selling Categories Insight:

Barista Espresso had the highest sales at $91,406.20.
Brewed Chai Tea and Gourmet Brewed Coffee also performed well in both quantity and revenue — showing they are customer favorites.

📈 Category Contribution Insight:

Coffee (39%) and Tea (28%) together contribute 67% of total sales.
These categories dominate the product mix — crucial for stocking strategy, marketing campaigns, and vendor negotiations.
